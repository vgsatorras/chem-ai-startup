# Company Principles

This repo is the whole company: business plan, pitch, science, and code. These principles guide every decision in it, whether a person or an AI agent makes it. When in doubt, re-read this file.

## The Motto

> **"Everything should be made as simple as possible, but not simpler."** (often attributed to Albert Einstein)

Every other principle below serves this one.

## Simplicity

- **KISS / YAGNI**: Don't build it until you need it.
- **The Algorithm (SpaceX)**: Question every requirement, delete parts, simplify, then speed up, then automate, in that order.
- **Gall's Law**: Complex systems that work evolve from simple systems that worked.
- **Do one thing well (Unix)**: Small pieces with clear purposes.
- **Focus means saying no (Apple)**: Say no to a thousand good ideas.

## Customers

- **Make something people want (YC)**: Nothing else matters if we fail at this.
- **Customer Obsession (Amazon)**: Start from the chemist's problem and work backwards.
- **Focus on the user and all else will follow (Google)**.
- **Genchi Genbutsu (Toyota)**: Go and see the real problem yourself, in the lab.

## Science & Truth

- **First-principles thinking**: Reason from physics and chemistry, not by analogy.
- **The lab is ground truth**: A model only counts once experiments validate it.
- **Reproducibility**: Every result can be rerun from this repo.
- **Dive Deep (Amazon)**: Know the details and trust data over anecdotes.
- **Measure what matters**: A few honest metrics, not many vanity ones.

## Execution

- **Bias for Action (Amazon)**: Speed matters. Most decisions are reversible, so decide fast; save deep deliberation for the irreversible ones.
- **Ship early and iterate (Reid Hoffman)**: If v1 doesn't embarrass you, you shipped too late.
- **Kaizen (Toyota)**: Small, continuous improvements.
- **Learn and Be Curious (Amazon)**: Never stop learning; adopt powerful new tools and trends fast.
- **Insist on the Highest Standards (Amazon)**: Simple doesn't mean sloppy.
- **Frugality (Amazon)**: Constraints breed resourcefulness.
- **Stay default alive (Paul Graham)**: Reach profitability before the money runs out.

## People & Culture

- **Ownership (Amazon)**: Think long term and never say "not my job."
- **Freedom and Responsibility (Netflix)**: Context, not control.
- **Radical transparency (Bridgewater)**: The best idea wins, not the highest title.
- **Candor (Pixar)**: Honest feedback, early and often.
- **Write it down (Amazon / Stripe)**: Clear writing is clear thinking.

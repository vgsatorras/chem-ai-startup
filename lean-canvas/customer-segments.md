### Early adopters
Characteristics: they **already run HTE or automation**, have a data-science team, and **publicly name synthesis as a bottleneck**.
- **Already buying AI for synthesis**: Pfizer (PostEra), Bayer Crop Science (Iktos), Zeon (Chemify). Iktos reports ~60 collaborations (Servier, Janssen, Pfizer, Ono).
- **HTE leaders**: AstraZeneca, Merck/MSD, Novartis, Roche, Lilly.
- **Agrochemical majors**: explicit and rising synthesis cost.

### Where to start (inference)
1. **Agrochemical discovery and pharma HTE groups**: quantified synthesis cost, existing HTE data, and budget for pilots.
2. **CROs as a leverage channel**: they publish volumes and success rates, so an improvement is measurable, but they will want proof first.
3. **Caution**: the field is crowded (Chemify, Molecule.one, Lila, Iktos, PostEra), and no buyer has yet been shown to pay for reaction-level prediction on new chemistry.

# Mission

Accurately model chemical reactions in a computer.

If we can predict whether a reaction works, under which conditions, and why, many downstream problems follow: synthesis routes, reaction conditions, catalyst screening, scale-up, waste, and uses nobody has defined yet.

## Milestones
Each milestone is a business that pays its way and moves us closer to the mission. Each has its own [Lean Canvas](lean-canvas/lean-canvas.md).

1. **Routes that work in the lab**: predict full synthesis routes for hard molecules, with conditions and catalyst for every step. ([canvas](lean-canvas/lean-canvas.md))
